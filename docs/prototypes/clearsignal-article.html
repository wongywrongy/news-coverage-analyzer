Netanyahu to meet Trump Wednesday about Iran nuclear talks

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu will meet with President Donald Trump in Washington on Wednesday to discuss U.S.-Iran nuclear negotiations. Netanyahu moved up his planned February 18 meeting following the conclusion of talks in Oman on Friday.

Background

Context

The meeting comes after U.S.-Iran nuclear negotiations concluded in Oman on Friday, with President Trump characterizing the talks as 'very good' and suggesting a deal is possible. Netanyahu's decision to move up his originally planned February 18 visit signals the urgency Israel places on being involved in any nuclear agreement discussions.

Israel has long opposed nuclear deals with Iran that it considers insufficiently restrictive. Netanyahu is expected to push for ballistic missile limitations and an end to Iranian regional support for proxy groups as conditions for any agreement — positions Iran has reportedly rejected.

The timing is notable: by meeting before the next round of negotiations rather than after, Netanyahu positions Israel to influence the U.S. negotiating posture rather than react to outcomes.
